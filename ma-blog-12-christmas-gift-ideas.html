Christmas Gift Ideas for Little Readers

Ideas for ages 2 to 6, a few for the dog lovers, and a free game you can play on Christmas Day.

10th October 2026 Maverick's Adventures 4 min read

Every year around this time, the same question lands in a lot of family group chats: what do you buy a two, three, four or five year old that they'll actually use, and that won't be forgotten by Boxing Day?

We can't claim to have the perfect answer for every child. But we do know what has worked in our own house, with our own cockapoo, and a few simple ideas that tend to go down well with little ones and with the grown-ups who read to them. Here is our gift guide for little readers and the dog lovers who love them.

“The best presents for small children are often the ones that get you sitting down together.”

Why Books Make a Brilliant Gift

A picture book isn't just something to open. It's something to share, again and again. At this age children love repetition, and a rhyming story they can start to join in with becomes part of the bedtime routine very quickly. It also means no batteries, no screens and no clutter.

For grandparents, aunties, uncles and godparents, a book is also one of the few gifts you can give and then take part in yourself, whether that's reading it over a video call or curled up on the sofa on Christmas Day.

Gift Ideas for Ages 2 to 6

Here are a few ideas that work well at this age, whatever your budget.

  • A rhyming picture book they can join in with. Short, repetitive and full of things to spot on each page. These are the books children ask for night after night.
  • A book plus a cuddly toy. Pairing a favourite story with a soft toy that matches it makes a present that feels special, and it gives bedtime a little extra magic.
  • Colouring and activity sheets. A good stocking filler and a lifesaver on a wet afternoon. We have a free printable pack on the site (more on that below).
  • A Christmas Eve box. A new book, a pair of pyjamas and the ingredients for a hot chocolate, opened the night before. It's a lovely tradition and it helps little ones wind down.
  • A book for the whole family to share. Pick something with a story the grown-ups enjoy reading out loud as much as the children enjoy hearing it.

For the Dog Lovers

If there's a dog lover in your life, whether that's a child who is obsessed with puppies or an adult who treats their dog like a family member (we are guilty), a gift with a bit of dog personality in it always lands well.

A Little Christmas Game

Here's something you can try with any copy of our book, and it costs nothing. Where is Maverick? is a hide-and-seek story, so why not turn it into a real one?

🐾 Try this on Christmas Day

Hide a small toy dog somewhere in the room before you start reading. Then, each time Maverick turns up on a page, let your little one run off and find the toy. By the last page, you'll have a very proud hide-and-seek champion and a tired, happy child.
