Maverick Has Gone Global!

Until this week, every copy of Where is Maverick? had gone to a reader right here in the UK. That changed a few days ago.

2nd October 2026 Maverick's Adventures 3 min read

Until this week, every single copy of Where is Maverick? had gone to a reader right here in the UK. That changed a few days ago, when someone outside the UK bought the book for the very first time.

It might sound like a small thing. One book, one sale. But for a story that started with a cockapoo hiding behind the sofa in our living room, watching it travel somewhere we've never been feels like a genuinely big moment.

“A little story about a cheeky cockapoo, created at home with a lot of love, is now making its way beyond the UK and into the wider world.”

Where It All Started

We've written before about how Where is Maverick? came together. A real dog with a habit of disappearing into the smallest spaces, a rhyming story built around his hide-and-seek games, and a lot of evenings spent getting the words and the pictures right. When we published it, we hoped a few local families might enjoy it.

If you missed it, you can read the full story of how the book went from an idea to an Amazon listing.

Read: How We Created Where is Maverick? →

We didn't think about it going anywhere beyond that. So the first sale from outside the UK genuinely caught us off guard, in the best way.

The Numbers So Far

In keeping with how we've always talked about this (we'd rather be honest about where things stand than polish the numbers), here's exactly where Maverick is at right now.

10UK Copies Sold
1International Copy
7 ★Reader Reviews

Eleven copies total isn't a huge number by most measures. But one of them travelling outside the UK, with zero advertising spend behind it, tells us something we didn't know for sure until now: Maverick's story can resonate with families well beyond our little corner of the world.

🐾 What this means for what's next

Practically, not much changes today. We're still the same small, home-grown operation we've always been. But it's given us a genuine boost heading into the next phase of growing this properly: more content, a more active Instagram, TikTok and Facebook, and getting back to writing here on the blog regularly.

Thank You

To everyone who has bought the book, left a review, or sent us a message to say their little one asks for it every night: thank you. Every single one of you is part of how a home-made story about a real dog ended up somewhere we never expected it to reach.

And who knows where Maverick might end up next? From our little corner of the UK to wherever his paws take him, this already feels like only the beginning.
